# Dataset Card: Five-Class Everyday Objects

## Status

Draft until final audit: **do not claim 1,000 images until verified**.

## Intended use

Fine-tuning and evaluation of closed-set object detection for bottle, chair, cup,
laptop, and cell phone. Not for person identification, surveillance, safety-critical
decisions, or commercial deployment.

## Composition

Record verified image, instance, session, negative-image, and per-class counts here.
Describe train/validation/test counts and confirm sessions do not cross splits.

## Collection and annotation

Document dates, locations at a non-identifying level, camera types, permissions,
annotation tool/version, labelling rules, reviewer process, and audit output.

## Variation, bias, and limitations

Summarize lighting, distance, angle, background, occlusion, blur, object styles,
geographic/environmental limitations, class imbalance, and likely blind spots.

## Privacy, licence, and retention

Record privacy review, consent/approval status, source licences, access controls,
retention period, deletion method, and whether redistribution is permitted.
